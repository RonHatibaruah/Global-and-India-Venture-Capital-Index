### 🧵 Story 10: Default Alive vs. Default Dead
```text
Paul Graham coined the phrase, but most founders still ignore it.

When you raise a $2M seed round, you have two clocks ticking:
1. Your cash balance going down.
2. Your revenue growing to break-even.

If clock #2 reaches break-even before clock #1 hits zero, you are "Default Alive" — you control your destiny.

If clock #1 hits zero first, you're "Default Dead" and forced into a predatory bridge round.

Runway is leverage. Protect it with your life. ⏳
```

### 🧵 Story 12: Why Top VCs Care About Bottoms-Up TAM
```text
Founder in a pitch: "The global logistics market is $8 Trillion. If we capture just 0.1%, we are an $8 Billion company!"

The VC tuned out immediately.

Top-down TAM is lazy.

Bottoms-up TAM wins:
"There are 42,000 mid-market freight brokers in India & SE Asia. Our ACV is $24,000/yr. That makes our reachable SAM $1.008 Billion today."

Arithmetic builds conviction. Big macro numbers build skepticism. 📐
```
