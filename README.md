# Global Venture Capital (VC) Funds Index & Intelligence Directory

> Comprehensive ranking and global directory of the top Venture Capital (VC) funds globally, categorized by market prestige tiers, investment stages, regional focus, Assets Under Management (AUM), and their track record of backing generation-defining tech companies.

---

## ⚡ Key Features

- **Prestige Tier Categorization**:
  - **Tier 1: Elite Sovereigns** (Sequoia, a16z, Benchmark, Founders Fund, Accel, Kleiner Perkins, Bessemer, Lightspeed, Index, General Catalyst)
  - **Tier 2: Multi-Stage Giants** (Insight Partners, Khosla, Thrive, Tiger Global, Coatue, NEA, Addition, Battery, IVP, Redpoint, Norwest, SoftBank)
  - **Tier 3: Early-Stage Champions** (First Round, Initialized, SV Angel, Felicis, Forerunner, Floodgate, Matrix)
  - **Tier 4: Deep Tech & Frontier** (Lux Capital, 8VC, DCVC, Flagship Pioneering, ARCH Venture Partners)
  - **Tier 5: Corporate Strategic VCs** (GV / Google Ventures, Salesforce Ventures, Intel Capital)
  - **Tier 6: Global Regional Powerhouses** (Peak XV, Balderton, Atomico, Kaszek, Creandum, Northzone, EQT Ventures, LocalGlobe & Latitude, 500 Global)

- **Comprehensive Multi-Dimensional Data**:
  - Global Ranking & Prestige Scores (1-100)
  - Assets Under Management (AUM) ($980B+ total aggregate tracked)
  - Historical Return Profiles & Generation-Defining Exits (Apple, Google, Facebook, Stripe, SpaceX, Airbnb, OpenAI, Uber, Spotify, etc.)
  - Active Unicorn & Frontier AI Portfolio (OpenAI, Anthropic, Databricks, Scale AI, Figma, Ramp, etc.)
  - General Partners (GPs) & Key Decision Makers
  - Typical Check Size Ranges & Regional Office Footprints

- **Interactive Intelligence Tools**:
  - **Side-by-Side Comparison Matrix**: Compare up to 4 funds side-by-side across AUM, stages, check sizes, and track record.
  - **Analytics & Leaderboard View**: Visual charts for AUM distribution, regional capital concentration, and syndicate overlap.
  - **Target List & CRM Export**: Star and bookmark target VC funds for fundraising pipelines with one-click export to CSV & JSON.
  - **Deep-Linking & SEO**: URL routes for `/fund/:fundSlug` and dynamic Schema.org FinancialService JSON-LD.
